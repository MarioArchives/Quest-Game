New personal missions (tampering, for the Manchester pub crawl; title: what to do, then the proof):
Hidden faces: Point out a face hiding in something ordinary (a plug socket, a tap, the foam on a pint) and get someone to agree they can see it too. Proof: a photo of the face, and who agreed.
Sign of the times: Get someone to read a sign, notice or chalkboard in the bar out loud to you. Proof: who read it, and what it said.
Busy bee: Get someone to tell you why there are bees all over Manchester. Proof: who told you, and their answer.
Not a mirror: Get someone to check their reflection in anything but a mirror: a spoon, a window, a phone screen. Proof: who did it, and what they used.
Colour match: Get someone to tell you your outfit matches something in the bar. Proof: who said it, and what matched.
The line-up: Get three people to stand against a wall with you for a photo, like a police line-up, without telling them why. Proof: the photo.
Hardboiled: Talk like a hardboiled detective ("the night was dark, and so was the stout") until someone comments on it. Proof: who noticed, and what they said.
Strike a pose: Get someone to copy the pose of a person in a picture, poster or painting in the bar. Proof: a photo of them next to it.
Rainbow line: Get three people in different colours to stand in rainbow order for a photo, without telling them why. Proof: the photo.
Human alphabet: Get someone to make a letter of the alphabet with their arms. Proof: a photo, or a player who saw it.
Shadow play: Get someone to make a shadow puppet on the wall with you, by phone torch. Proof: a photo of the shadow.
Chalk outline: Lay out the outline of a body on your table in beer mats, napkins or straws, and get someone to ask what it is. Proof: a photo, and who asked.
Where's Bob?: Hide your miniature Bob somewhere in the bar and get another player to find him. Proof: who found him, and where.
Last words: Get someone to guess Bob's last words. Proof: who guessed, and what they said.
Front page: Get someone to come up with tomorrow's newspaper headline about tonight. Proof: who said it, and the headline.
Jury of one: Tell a stranger about Bob's murder in one sentence and get them to point at a suspect in your group. Proof: who they picked, and a player who heard it.
Beer mat haiku: Get someone to write a line of poetry on a beer mat for you. Proof: the beer mat.
Manc lesson: Get a local to teach you a bit of Manc slang, then use it on another player without explaining it. Proof: the word, and who you used it on.
Good deed: Do something small and kind for a stranger (hold a door, find them a seat, pay them a compliment) and get a thank-you. Proof: what you did, and a player who saw it.
Thank-you note: Leave the bar staff a thank-you note on a napkin when they're not rushed, and get a smile out of them. Proof: a photo of the note, or a player who saw it.
