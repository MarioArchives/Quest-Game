# Demo clips

Short clips for the demo game's video entries (the mock backend serves them; `npm run seed -- --demo` uploads them to
the Storage emulator). They never ship with a real game. Each is a few seconds of a Wikimedia Commons video, cropped
to a phone's portrait frame, scaled to 360×640, re-encoded as H.264 without sound, with a still (`.jpg`) from its first
second. The trimmed clips keep their source's licence.
