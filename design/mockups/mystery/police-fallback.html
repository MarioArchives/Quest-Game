When the police can’t get through

Deciding: what a police check-in does when Twilio can’t ring a player. Either the service fails part-way through the night (out of credit, a key or geo-permission error, the webhook unreachable, calls that never connect), or a player’s number can’t be set up (none to give, won’t verify, a landline, abroad, or they’d rather not share it). Five ways to handle both, each with the player’s side, the GM’s side and the rule that keeps fines fair.

APatched through · per call · automatic

Option A · per call · nothing to switch

A call that can’t get through is re-sent to the Board, by itself.

Each call stands alone. When Twilio can’t connect one, the same check-in opens on the Board with a push and a fresh 5 minutes. A player whose number doesn’t work gets every check-in on the Board. The GM doesn’t have to do anything.

Ring 07700 900412 Never connected Board + push 5:00 starts again +5 / −5 as today
Detect
Per call: Twilio refuses it, it ends failed or canceled, or no status comes back within 90 s. A watchdog in the timer catches the silent ones.
Player gets
Today’s card with a “didn’t connect” slip, a toast if the app is open and a push if it isn’t. Same question, a new 5-minute window.
Fines
Never connected: no fine; the phone attempt is logged, never scored. Rang out, declined or voicemail: missed, −5 as today, and not re-sent. A missed re-sent card: −5 as today; the GM can waive any fine from the log.
No number
None given, declined, landline or abroad: every check-in is on the Board from the start. A number whose first call never connects is marked “Didn’t connect” on My file and stays on the Board until it’s changed.
Back to phone
Automatic: the next call tries the phone again. A marked number is tried again once it’s saved again.

DatapoliceCalls.channel: 'phone' | 'app' policeCalls.fallbackReason: 'never-connected' | 'no-number' | null policeCalls.phone: { sid, status, errorCode, at } policeCalls.waived phones/{uid}.status: 'ok' | 'failed' · a re-send moves endsAt; the call stays open.

Cost

The smallest build: the card exists. Adds the status webhook, a 90 s watchdog in runPolice, the slip and a waive button.

Risk

In a real outage every call still tries the phone first, so every check-in lands up to 90 s late, and the GM only finds out by reading the log.

BThe switchboard · game-wide · circuit breaker

Option B · the whole game · one switch

Three dead calls in a row switch everyone to the Board until the line recovers.

Mid-night failures are rarely one phone: credit, keys, geo permission and the webhook break for everyone at once. So the switchboard watches the line for the whole game. It trips once, tells everyone once, and comes back once.

3 never connect Trips Everyone on the Board Check every 15 min Next call on probation Lines back
Detect
Across the game: 3 never-connected calls in a row to at least 2 players, or at once on an account error (20003, 21215, no credit). A no-answer counts neither way: it rang. The GM gets one push: “Switchboard tripped”.
Player gets
A “Phone lines down” banner on the Board and a feed post; check-ins arrive as today’s card with a push. Nobody is rung meanwhile.
Fines
The calls that tripped it are re-sent with a fresh window, no fine. While down, today’s in-app rule: miss it, −5. A no-answer before the trip still counts, because the phone rang.
No number
Shown on the switchboard as “No line”; that player is on the Board all night, whatever the breaker says. The GM can test-call a number and put it on the line.
Back to phone
Every 15 min it checks Twilio without ringing anyone (account, credit, webhook ping). When all three pass, the next due call goes by phone on probation: if it connects, the lines are back; if not, it trips again. Or the GM test-calls their own phone and taps Restore, or keeps the Board for the night.

Datagame/switchboard: { mode: 'phone' | 'board' | 'probation', since, reason, errorCode, streak, checkedAt } (GM reads, functions write) game/state.policeLine: 'phone' | 'board' (for the banner) policeCalls.channel fallbackReason: 'lines-down' | 'never-connected' | 'no-number' phones/{uid}.status

Cost

Medium: breaker state in the timer, the health check, a Switchboard folder, the banner and a feed post.

Risk

One bad number mustn’t trip it, hence the 2-player rule. A wrong trip keeps everyone on the Board until the next check passes (15 min at most).

Option C · each player · decided before the night counts on it

Each player picks Phone or App only on My file, and the line is tested at setup.

Most number problems show up the first time Twilio rings, so ring once at setup. The test call reads out a code the player keys back, which proves the number, the caller ID, geo permission and the webhook in one go. Whatever fails lands on App only, said plainly.

Detect
At setup, the test call. Mid-game, per player: 2 never-connected calls in a row pause that player’s line. If 3 lines pause within 10 min the GM gets a push: it’s Twilio, not the players.
Player gets
A Police line folder on My file, stamped Phone ✓, App only, Didn’t verify or Paused. A pause or a failed test moves them to App by itself, with a push, a tag on the Board and a “Ring me to test” button.
Fines
Never-connected calls are re-sent to the Board with a fresh window, no fine. App only is no penalty and no bonus: same questions, same points. No answer on a verified phone: −5 as today.
No number
None, declined, landline or abroad: App only from the start, with no nagging. The test call finds landlines and foreign numbers before the game does.
Back to phone
Per player: one good test call (“Ring me to test”, or the GM’s “Test line”) puts them back on Phone.

Medium: the setup folder, a test call with a code (about $0.03 each), the statuses, a GM column and a Board tag.

App only is easier (a calculator, no pub noise), so the GM may want persons of interest on Phone. A full outage is found one player at a time.

DSecond ring · per call · retry ladder

Option D · per call · kind to loud pubs

Ring, ring once more, then the Board with a longer window.

A pub is loud and phones live in pockets. Instead of fining the first missed ring, the call climbs a ladder: a second ring two minutes later, then the Board with 10 minutes. The same ladder catches calls that never connect.

Ring · 25 s No answer or dead Ring again · +2 min Board + push · 10 min Missed all 3: −5 once
Detect
Per call: each rung ends answered, no answer or never connected. An account error (auth, geo, credit) skips straight to the Board, since retrying won’t help.
Player gets
Missed calls from Blackwood Manor PD (the vCard puts it in their contacts), then a push and the Board card listing what was tried and the new deadline: 10 minutes from the Board rung.
Fines
Nothing is fined until the last rung runs out. A missed ring is forgiven if they answer on the Board; never-connected rungs never count. Miss all three: −5, once.
No number
The ladder starts on the Board rung with the usual 5 minutes, so no one waits on rings that can’t happen.
Back to phone
Nothing to restore: every call starts on rung 1. When 3 calls in an hour reach the Board the GM gets one push, with “Board only for 1 hr”.

DatapoliceCalls.rungs: [{ channel: 'phone' | 'app', at, result: 'answered' | 'no-answer' | 'never-connected', errorCode }] policeCalls.channel (where it was answered) · endsAt moves per rung · settings policeRetryMin: 2 policeBoardWindowMin: 10 · phones/{uid}.status

Cost

Small to medium: the timer drives the rungs. Up to 2 calls per check-in, but unanswered calls aren’t billed.

Risk

A check-in can run 15 min, half of a person of interest’s 30-min cycle. Two rings two minutes apart is a lot. In a full outage every call still burns two attempts.

EPolice radio · in the app · the GM’s voice

Option E · in the app · still a call

When the phone can’t ring, the app does: the GM’s voice and an on-screen keypad.

The fallback doesn’t have to be a form. The radio plays the recordings the phone would (intro, question, verdict) inside the app and takes the answer on a keypad. A player with no number, or a night without Twilio, still gets a call from the police.

Never connected · no number Radio rings + push Pick up: voice + subtitles Key it, # Verdict clip
Detect
Per call, as in A. Players with no working number are on the radio from the start.
Player gets
An incoming call from Blackwood Manor PD inside the app: it rings if the app is open, and a push brings them in if not. Pick up plays the clips with subtitles; they key the answer and press #.
Fines
Never connected: the radio rings with a fresh 5 minutes, no fine. Radio not picked up in time, or nothing keyed: −5, the same as a phone no-answer. Subtitles mean a muted phone isn’t a fail.
No number
The radio is their line, and My file says so: “Your line: police radio”. Nothing to set up, nothing to decline.
Back to phone
Per call: the next check-in tries the phone first. The GM can put everyone on the radio in one tap (out of credit, say) and back.

DatapoliceCalls.channel: 'phone' | 'radio' fallbackReason policeCalls.clips (what was played, so both say the same) settings.policeChannel: 'phone-first' | 'radio' phones/{uid}.status · clips in public/audio/police/, already planned

Cost

The biggest build: audio playback, the ringing screen and the keypad. No Twilio cost, and it could ship before Twilio does.

Risk

Audio needs a tap (Pick up is that tap). On iPhone, push only reaches the installed app. Pub noise: the subtitles carry it.

Recommendation

B, the switchboard, with A’s re-send and C’s line status inside it.

Fits how Twilio actually fails, keeps every fine fair, and asks nothing of a GM who’s busy playing.

Why

  1. The failure is game-wide, so the switch should be too. Credit, keys, geo permission and the webhook break for everyone at once. B notices after three calls, tells the GM once, and its health check brings the phones back without ringing anyone.
  2. Fair by construction. Any call that never connected is re-sent with a fresh window and never fined; a ring that went unanswered still is, because the player had their chance.
  3. For numbers that can’t be set up, take only the status from C: phones/{uid}.status and an honest “App only”. Add the test call later if bad numbers turn out to be common.
  4. Keep E for later. It’s the best next look for the Board card, and it makes the fallback feel like the real thing.
