Mystery Pub Crawl · design decision · tip-offs

How does a trusted confidant notice they can tip off the police?

Today the only way in is a red Tip off the police about X button at the bottom of another player’s file, so a confidant has to open someone’s file to find out they can tip at all, and nothing says when the hour is up. Five directions make the ready moment noticeable from the Board and put the tip one or two taps away. In every one, the button on a player’s file stays as a second way in.

The rules, as built
  • ConfidantScore above +10 (the GM moves the line). Exactly +10 is not enough: +11 is.
  • How oftenOne tip every 60 min, timed from your last tip, even if you lose and regain the standing.
  • The noteRequired: what they’ve been up to, 1–200 characters. The feed post names you and shows it.
  • WhoNot yourself, not a player who has left, not anyone whose check-in is already open (refused without using your tip).
  • Their sideA push and a police check-in: a sum like 7 × 8 within 5 min. Right +5, wrong or missed −5.
A

The light on your points chip

A police light rides on the chip in the app bar: lit when you can tip, dark during the hour, unlit for everyone else.

Recommended

The idea

A police light joins your points chip on every screen. It flashes when your tip line opens, with a one-off toast, then glows until you tip. Tap it: pick a suspect from a sheet of mugshots, then write the note in today’s sheet.

What’s good

Seen from anywhere, including the Map, and it sits next to the score that earns it. Two taps to the note. During the hour, a ring drains around the dark light, and a tap shows the exact time.

Cost and risk

About 34px more in a full app bar, so the title truncates sooner. A steady red glow is easy to tune out, so it only pulses for the first 10 s. The unlit light for non-confidants is a hint, but it’s chrome on everyone’s screen.

B

A tip line on the Feed

While your line is open, a Form 7 folder sits at the top of the Feed with the suspects’ faces on it.

The idea

A Form 7 folder is pinned at the top of the Feed while your tip line is open, with the suspects’ faces on it. Tap a face, write the note inside the folder and send. Then it folds into a one-line fuse.

What’s good

The Board is where everyone lands, so it’s hard to miss, and one tap picks who. No new chrome. The cooldown is one quiet line, and non-confidants get a strip they can dismiss.

Cost and risk

Pushes the live missions down while it’s open, and your own police check-in still has to go above it. Nothing shows on the Map or My file, so it needs a dot on the Board tab there.

C

Point the finger from the standings

The standings become the tip line: a red TIP tab at the end of each row you may tip.

The idea

While your line is open, every suspect you may tip gets a red TIP tab at the end of their row. Dashed lines at +10 and −10 mark where you become a trusted confidant or a person of interest, and a tip reuses today’s note sheet.

What’s good

You choose from the scores you’re already reading, so the choice is informed. The rule is drawn on the chart, which doubles as the teaser: a dashed ghost bar shows how far you are from +11.

Cost and risk

The Feed is the default tab, so the ready moment depends on a flag on the Standings tab (and a dot on the Board tab elsewhere). That makes it the easiest of the five to miss. The TIP column crowds bars near +25.

D

The whistle

An event when the line opens, then a floating whistle until you use it; the tip is a line-up and a typed statement.

The idea

Make the moment an event. When the line opens, a TIP LINE OPEN stamp lands once (or a push arrives, if the phone is in a pocket). After that, a red whistle floats on every screen until you use it. It opens a line-up, and the note is a typed witness statement.

What’s good

The hardest to miss, and the push reaches you mid-pint. The whistle disappears during the hour, so nothing nags, and the buzz tells you when it’s back. Pure theatre that suits the pack.

Cost and risk

The most to build: the stamp moment, the push, and a full-screen line-up. A floating button covers feed posts and the Map’s controls. Between tips the only clock is on My file, and non-confidants learn the rule only from tip posts.

E

The tip pad

Tip-offs are slips on a tear-off pad at the top of My file; a fresh one pokes out of the My file tab.

The idea

Tips are slips on a tear-off pad at the top of My file. When your line opens, a slip pokes up out of the My file tab. Circle a face, write what you saw on the slip, and tear it off.

What’s good

Tactile and in-world. One place holds the slip, the cooldown dial and the sealed pad that tells everyone else the rule. It’s noticeable without glowing or moving.

Cost and risk

It lives away from the home screen, so it’s one tap further away, and the slip is small next to the Map. My file already leads with witness requests, and the pad pushes the suspect folder down.

My pick: A

A, the light on the points chip, because it is the only direction that shows the ready state on every screen (Board, Map, My file and anyone’s file) without moving content, and the tip is two taps away. It is also the cheapest to build: one chip segment and one picker in front of today’s note sheet.

Take two things from the others: D’s single push (“Your tip line is open”) for a phone in a pocket, and C’s dashed +10 line on the standings as the teaser for everyone who isn’t a confidant yet, in place of A’s unlit light.
