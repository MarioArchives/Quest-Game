The ideaGroup the eleven into four plain places and show them as folder tabs, so the GM always sees where everything is. Tonight is the inbox.

What’s goodNothing hides behind a menu, and every place is one tap away. It reuses today’s folders and tags, so it’s the smallest real build: the locker splits into four views.

Cost / riskA second row of tabs above the content (the bottom bar is the app’s). Labels must stay one word; a fifth tab would not fit at 390 px.

The ideaDon’t send the GM to a console to act on the night; put the GM’s buttons on the screens everyone already uses.

What’s goodNothing to learn: the GM rules where they’re looking. The GM tab gets short and calm, and suspects’ files finally show the GM their whole story.

Cost / riskThe biggest build: GM layers on the Board, the files and the Map. GM-only words (statements, frames) on shared screens are easy to show over a shoulder. A GM who plays sees two jobs mixed.

Recommendation: A, four folder tabs, with B’s list on Tonight

A fixes the actual complaint. Eleven hidden places become four named tabs that are always on screen, and the five jobs sit on the first one with their buttons, so the alarms can’t hide in a section. Everything else is one tap away. It reuses the locker’s folders, tags and engine sections, so it’s mostly regrouping, not new screens.

Take from B the order (harm first) and “Later, 15 min” on a story mission. Keep C’s search in mind if Setup grows. D’s “coming next” rows (police at 22:05, the next drop) would fit at the foot of Tonight. Leave E: it’s the most work, and it puts GM-only words on shared screens.
