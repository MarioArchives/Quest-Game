Missions, the map and the feed: five ways to split the Board

What’s being decided

The Board becomes Missions and holds only missions (group, story and periodic) plus a new folder tab, Map, where the Map bottom tab was. The feed of everything else the game logs gets that bottom tab.

Each option answers five questions: how the three kinds of mission share the tab, how a full-screen map lives inside a folder tab, where Standings goes, what the Feed looks like alone, and the bottom tabs.

A

Three folder tabs

Today’s Board with the Map moved in and the feed moved out: Missions · Map · Standings

Standings · stay a folder tab beside the missions, as today. Bottom tabs · Missions (the magnifier) · Feed · My file.

The ideaThe least change: Board becomes Missions and gains the Map as a folder tab; the timeline goes to its own bottom tab, exactly as it is.

What’s goodNothing new to learn and the least code: Board.tsx gets a third tab, TimelineFeed loses the binder and the roll-call tags, the Map route moves.

Cost / riskThree folder tabs under a bottom tab, the first one named like its parent. The map sits two levels down, and Standings is the odd one out among the missions.

B

One binder for every kind

Roll calls join the binder: a divider tab for every mission of the night, whatever its kind

Standings · move to the top of the Feed as one line of faces. Bottom tabs · Missions (a folder) · Feed (a paper) · My file.

The ideaEvery mission of the night in the binder you already have. Periodic missions become tabs like the rest, so there is one place and one rule for “needs you”.

What’s goodThe binder is built and known. Roll calls stop being a separate card stack and keep their history. Standings sit where you read what moved them.

Cost / riskAbout 14 tabs by midnight (four roll calls): slim tabs from the fourth. With another tab open, a roll call’s urgency is only a red edge. The standings strip and filters are new.

C

Needs you, then the docket

One card for what needs you soonest, then every mission filed by kind, the story as chapters

Standings · hang off your points chip in the app bar, on every screen. Bottom tabs · Missions (a stopwatch) · Log · My file.

The ideaTriage first, then the record. The top card is whatever needs you soonest, of any kind; under it the night is filed by kind, so the murder plot reads as chapters.

What’s goodThe most urgent thing is always first and full size. Each kind gets a shape and a history. “Two of seven chapters” builds suspense for nothing. Standings are one tap from anywhere.

Cost / riskA mission shows in two places (the card and its row), so their states must agree. The docket and the drop-down are new. New players may not guess that the chip opens the standings.

D

On the clockRecommended

Every open mission as a ticket, soonest deadline first, whatever its kind; Feed and Standings pair up

Standings · a folder tab of the Feed: Feed · Standings, today’s Board pair. Bottom tabs · Missions (a target) · Feed (footprints) · My file (a card).

The ideaMissions ordered by the clock, not by kind or number. The two bottom tabs mirror each other: Missions is what to do and where, Feed is what happened and where it left everyone.

What’s goodThe next deadline is always on top, which is the game’s real pressure (fines). No binder to learn; story, random and roll call read the same way. Feed · Standings is the Board’s code, moved.

Cost / riskA long-window mission that fires late lands lower down (a “just in” stamp and the tab dot must carry it). A story mission is only its stamp, so the plot has less presence. The ribbon is new.

E

The map is the desk

The missions folder lies on the map: Missions pulls it up, Map slides it down; one mission at a time, as a deck

Standings · move into My file, under your own suspect folder. Bottom tabs · Missions (a pin) · Wire · My file (a card).

The ideaThe most physical answer to a full-screen map inside a folder tab: the map is the desk the case folder lies on, and the two tabs move the folder, not the page.

What’s goodSwitching to the map is instant and keeps your place. One mission per card means no tabs inside the folder. The Wire stays short because past stops fold away.

Cost / riskThe map loads whenever Missions opens (data, battery), and dragging a folder over Leaflet fights its gestures. A deck hides what is off-screen. Standings are two taps away.

Recommendation: D, on the clock

Deadlines are the game’s real pressure: every mission and roll call costs points if you miss it. D answers “what do I do next?” the same way for all three kinds, and the order only moves when a mission arrives or closes. Inside Missions the only tabs are Missions and Map, with no binder. Feed · Standings is the Board you have now minus the binder, so most of that half is a move, not a build.

Worth borrowing: C’s “story so far” (chapters, the rest sealed) for the filed spike, so the plot keeps its presence. Add B’s Feed filters once a night’s feed gets long. If the ribbon is too much new work, A’s map (today’s, with Now / Next at the foot) drops straight into D’s Map tab.
