A Manchester Murder: Pub Crawl · GM console · design decision

When does the case open?

Today the case sits in the lobby until the GM taps Open case on Tonight’s strip. New: the GM sets a start time (a date and time, London), suspects see a countdown, and at zero the case opens on its own. The GM can still open early, move the time (+15, +30 or a full picker), clear it back to opening by hand, and choose whether suspects hear about a move. Five places and shapes for it, each in the locker’s case-file look.

The rules, as proposed
  • Start timeOne date and time, London. Optional: with none, the GM opens the case by hand, as today.
  • CountdownSuspects see it from the moment it is set (their side is the other mockup, countdown.html).
  • At zeroThe case opens itself. A rule, not a switch (see the verdict).
  • Open nowAny time before zero; asks once while a time is set.
  • Move+15 / +30 or the full picker, only while the case is not open yet.
  • ClearBack to opening by hand; the countdown goes.
  • TellA move or a clear can push and post in the log book: “The case now opens at 19:30.” On by default once anyone has booked.
A

The time on the case strip

Tonight · strip + sheetThe start time is written on the case strip, where Open case is today. On the night the countdown, +15, +30 and Open now are one tap; tap the time for a plain sheet with the day and time. Recommended: nothing new to find, every night-of action in one place, and the cheapest to build.

Recommended

Why it works

The GM’s eyes are already on the strip all night. Nudges and Open now are one tap; the full picker is one more.

What it costs

The strip grows from one line to four while a time is set. It shrinks back once the case is open.

Build

Reuses CaseStrip, ConfirmSheet and the phone’s native date input behind “Other…”. One new setting: the start time.

B

A line in Setup

Setup · settings formThe start time is a setting, in a When it opens folder above the others in Setup, using the phone’s own date and time wheels. A standing Tell suspects when it moves switch replaces the per-move question. Tonight only shows the countdown and Open now.

Why it works

The time sits with the other rules of the night and uses inputs the phone already knows. Easy to set up days ahead.

What it costs

On the night, a +15 means Tonight → Setup → field → Save: four taps from a screen the GM isn’t on.

Build

A new settings field and folder; the save bar is new. The standing tell switch is one less question per move, and one more setting.

C

A tag on the twine

Tonight · kraft tagThe opening is a kraft tag hung first on Tonight’s twine, the time hand-written on it. Its fuse burns down through the last two hours; a move crosses the old time out in red pen and hangs a note to the suspects from the tag, waiting for Tell them or Keep it quiet.

Why it works

It is a job on the twine like the others, in the locker’s own look, and the crossed-out times tell the night’s story.

What it costs

Hand-writing is charming but slower to read than the strip at a glance. A second card on Tonight says “not open” twice.

Build

A new tag kind plus a writing overlay (the hand font is already in the pack, for the vote board). The fuse is CSS.

D

A clock you drag

Tonight · clock card + full screenA wall clock on felt under the strip. The red wedge is the time left; drag the red pin (or tap +15 / +30) to move it. Setting it is a full-screen dial. Open now is a 2-second hold, so it can’t go off in a pocket.

Why it works

Time left is a shape, readable from across the bar. Dragging a later time is one gesture, and the hold makes an early open deliberate.

What it costs

A 12-hour face needs Morning / Evening, and a far-off date doesn’t show on a dial. Dragging to the minute is fiddly with one thumb.

Build

The most new code: an SVG dial with drag and keyboard steps, a press-and-hold button, and a full-screen sheet.

E

The diary page

Setup · diary + Tonight · tear-off padIn Setup, a desk diary: circle the night, then write the opening into that day’s roster. On Tonight a tear-off day calendar counts down (“12 days” early on, hours on the night) and takes the nudges; at zero its page tears off. A move is stamped Tell them or Quietly.

Why it works

Picking a night weeks ahead is easiest on a calendar, and the pad gives “12 days” the same weight as “1 h 14 min”.

What it costs

Two places to look (diary in Setup, pad on Tonight). The roster is a lot of screen for a single entry.

Build

A month grid and roster are new components; the pad is CSS. Could later hold the route’s stop times too.

Pick A

The time on the case strip

On the night the GM is on Tonight, phone in one hand and a drink in the other. A puts every night-of action (the countdown, +15, +30, Open now, Clear) on the strip they already watch, one tap each, and keeps the date-and-time setting to one plain sheet.

  • Borrow the stamp from E for the tell choice if the toggle feels too quiet.
  • Ask before applying a nudge only once someone has booked; before that, just apply it.
  • Setup can hold a read-only line (“Opens Sat 7 Nov, 19:00 · change on Tonight”) so it can be found from there too.
The rule

“Opens on its own at zero” is the rule, not a toggle

The countdown is a promise to the suspects. A countdown that reaches zero and does nothing breaks it, and a toggle would allow exactly that state. The way out already exists: clear the time and it is back to opening by hand.

  • Not ready at 18:55? +15 is one tap from the strip.
  • A GM-only push ten minutes out (“The case opens itself in 10 min. +15?”) catches the GM who forgot.
  • Open now covers the early start; Pause still exists once it is open.

Build note: the timer runs every 5 minutes, so on its own it could open the case up to 5 minutes late while the suspects’ countdown says zero. Zero needs its own trigger (a task scheduled for the start time, or a one-minute check in the last hour).
